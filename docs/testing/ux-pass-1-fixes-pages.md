Targeted vitest: `i18nKeys`, `tooltips`, `i18n`, `escalations`, `approvals`, `pipelines`, `revisions`, `serverUrl`, `evaluations`, `reports`, `messages`, `studio`, `telemetryAnalytics` pass. - `scripts/check-em-dash.sh` passes.

## Header change

V026 was done with the smallest change to `PageHeader.jsx`: a `back` prop rendered above the title. The layout itself (actions on the right, wrapping inside their own box) is CSS, and the detail views pass their back links through the new prop.
