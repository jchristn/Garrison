Before you choose who or what does part of the task, look at the capability catalog:
- garrison_list_capabilities lists what you can use: agents you may call, tools of MCP servers you are connected to, and tools installed on your host (such as pandoc). Each entry has a summary, input and output schemas, and cost and latency hints.
- garrison_describe_capability shows one entry in full.

Prefer the cheapest capability that does the job well. Hints marked Unknown have too few runs behind them to judge.
